      Spotify Connect (full songs)

      Lets up to 5 Spotify Premium people play your Spotify playlists with full songs. One-time setup:

      1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and sign in with your Spotify account.
      2. Create app. Name: Craig's Songs. Description: anything. Check Web API.
      3. Redirect URI: paste this exactly, then click Add:
      4. Save, then copy the app's Client ID into the box below.
      5. In the app's Settings → User Management, add the name and Spotify email of each person who should get full songs (you included).

      Make as many edits as you like, then tap Save all. Changes go live on the site about a minute after saving.
