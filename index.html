              🎤 Make your own songs
              Rather have Craig make one for you? Fill out a quick form with who it's for and some fun details. 🎁 Request a song

              Every song in this app was made with an AI music app. You write (or describe) the words, pick a style, and it sings it for you, with music, vocals and all, in about a minute. Anyone can do it, and you can start free.

              1. Pick an app

              • Suno (what Craig uses). Free plan: 50 credits a day, enough for several songs a day, for personal use. Pro is about $8 a month for lots more songs, downloads and permission to use them commercially.
              • Udio. Similar idea, with a free plan to try it.
              • ElevenLabs Music. Paid plans from about $5 a month.

              Prices as of October 2026; they change, so check the app's own pricing page.

              2. Write the words

              • Write them yourself, or ask an AI chat app (like Claude or ChatGPT) for help: "Write a funny country song about my brother-in-law who always burns the burgers. Include his name, his dog Max, and the time he fell in the pool."
              • The more real details (names, nicknames, inside jokes, places), the better the song.
              • Label the parts in brackets: [Verse 1], [Chorus], [Bridge], [Outro]. The app follows them.

              3. Make it (Suno)

              1. Tap Create and switch to Custom mode.
              2. Paste your words into Lyrics.
              3. In Style, describe the sound: "upbeat 90s country, male vocals, fiddle, fun" or "pop punk, female vocals, fast".
              4. Give it a title and tap Create. You get two versions; keep your favorite or try again.

              4. Share it

              Every app has a share link you can text to people. Made one you'd like in this app? Send Craig the link.
